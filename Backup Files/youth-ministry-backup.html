Youth Ministry

Growing together in faith and purpose.

Who We Are

A place where young people can belong and grow.

Our youth ministry is a welcoming space for students and young adults to build lasting friendships, grow deeper in their relationship with God, and discover their purpose in Christ.

Through worship, teaching, prayer, and fellowship, we help each young person build a strong spiritual foundation and live boldly for Jesus.

Gathering Times

Make room for faith and friendship.

  • Sunday Lessons: Adults and youth, 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM
  • Friday Nights: 7:30 PM - 9:00 PM
  • Focused on worship, prayer, and encouragement
  • Open to youth and young adults

Growing in Truth

We explore the Bible ourselves.

We open God's Word with honest questions and curious hearts, learning how its truth connects to our everyday lives. Together, we discover what we believe and grow in confidence to follow Jesus.

Beyond Our Walls

We go out and share life together.

Fellowship gives us space to laugh, encourage one another, and build friendships that last. Whether we are serving, exploring, or enjoying a new place, we make memories while reflecting God's love.

Real Community

There is room to just be together.

Some of the best connections happen in the ordinary moments. We hang out, talk, play, and get to know one another outside of church so every friendship can grow naturally and everyone can feel at home.
