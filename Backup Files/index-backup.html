About Us

Growing together in faith and purpose.

At Iglesia Canaan, we believe in spiritual growth, discipleship, and building a welcoming place where families and individuals can encounter God. We are a church that is expanding with joy, faith, and a passion to see lives transformed.

We are especially committed to nurturing the next generation through our youth ministry and helping every person grow closer to God through prayer, worship, and community.

What We Believe

  • Jesus Christ is at the center of all we do.
  • Every person is welcome in the house of God.
  • We grow through prayer, worship, and community.
  • We are building a stronger future for our youth and church family.
